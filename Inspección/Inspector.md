# Inspector

Operational role responsible for on-site supervision of associates assigned to a hotel, arrival verification, uniform delivery, and investigation of reported cases.

## Responsibilities

- **Verifies the associate's arrival on Day 1** at the property (transition `White → Apple Green` in the [[Associate Status Indicator]]).
- **Delivers the uniform** to the associate when transitioning to `Light Blue` (Day 3+) in the [[Associate Status Indicator]].
- Supervises the on-site operation of associates at the hotel.
- Investigates cases of associates in `Red` status (reported by the [[Hotel/Area Manager|Area Manager]]) in the [[Associate Status Indicator]].
- **Has independent authority to decide** the outcome of the investigation, without the need for escalation:
  - `Black` ([[Core/Modules/Blacklist|Blacklist]]) — if the dispute is resolved in favor of the hotel.
  - `Dark Green` (reinstated) — if the dispute is resolved in favor of the associate.
- Supports the operation of the active client hotel ([[Onboarding Status Indicator#Orange — Signed agreement, active hotel client|Orange in Onboarding]]).
- Receives notification of a [[Core/Modules/Work Accident/Work Accident|workplace accident]] in their zone (see [[Core/Modules/Work Accident/Work Accident Flow|Workplace Accident Flow]]).
- Completes the accident card with medical follow-up information: transfer, diagnosis, days of incapacity, observations.
- Is the **final party responsible for closing** the accident card, once the information is complete.
- Upon closure, manages the associate's transition from `Gray → Dark Green` in the [[Associate Status Indicator]] when medical discharge is received.
- Consults the **Extended Lunch Indicator** in the [[Timesheet]] to identify associates whose lunch exceeds 30 minutes in their zone.
