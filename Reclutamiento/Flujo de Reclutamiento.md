# Recruitment Flow

Process by which [[Recruitment]] **captures, validates, and registers new associates** to feed the [[Associate Pool]]. This flow does **not** cover requisitions directly: its objective is to ensure the pool has available associates so the [[Requisition Flow]] can consume them.

> [!info] Connection point with the [[Requisition Flow]]
> The [[Associate Pool]] is the only point where both flows connect. The Recruitment Flow **feeds** the pool; the [[Requisition Flow]] **consumes** it.

## Nature of the Flow

The Recruitment Flow is **continuous**: [[Recruitment]] is **always hiring**, whether or not there are open requisitions. The objective is to keep the [[Associate Pool]] healthy and ready for when the [[Requisition Flow]] needs to consume it.

It does not depend on an external trigger — it runs permanently. Unmatched requisitions may **accelerate** or **prioritize** certain positions/zones, but they are not a condition for starting the flow.

## Phases

### Phase 1 — Initial Interview (recruitment panel)

Capture of the associate's initial information:
- Age
- Gender
- Full name
- Address
- Phone

### Phase 2 — Oranje App Registration (completed by the associate)

- SSN
- ITIN
- Position (see [[Positions]])
- English level (see [[English Levels]])
- Experience level
- Transportation type
- Modality (see [[Employment Modalities]])

### Phase 3 — Validation and Approval

[[Recruitment]] reviews the captured information and approves or rejects the associate.

### Phase 4 — Access Enablement

The approved associate is granted access to the corresponding panels.

## Output

The approved associate **enters the [[Associate Pool]]** with their [[Associate Status Indicator]] active. From there they become available to be assigned to a hotel through the [[Requisition Flow]].
