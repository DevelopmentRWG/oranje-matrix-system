# Recruitment Manager

Role responsible for coordinating the intake of requisitions and overseeing special cases within the [[Recruitment/Recruitment|Recruitment]] module.

## Responsibilities

- Monitors the shared [[Requisition|requisition]] inbox and intervenes manually only in exceptional cases: balancing between groups, absent team lead, and correcting assignment errors. Requisitions untaken for more than 24 hours are automatically assigned by the system.
- Reviews [[Core/Modules/Blacklist|Blacklist]] cases.
- Supervises the [[Recruiter Team Lead|Recruiter Team Leads]].
- Has visibility of the **Extended Lunch Indicator** in the [[Timesheet]] to evaluate behavioral patterns of active associates.
