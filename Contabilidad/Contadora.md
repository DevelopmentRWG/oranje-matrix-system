# Accountant

Operational role responsible for executing the weekly financial processes within the Oranje system: reviewing the Weekly Associate Summary, validating the Pre-Payroll, managing deductions, configuring internal rates, reconciling with the check provider, and calculating vacation pay.

## Responsibilities

- **Reviews the [[Weekly Associate Summary]]**: verifies that it is in order before sending it for approval by the [[Accounting Manager]].
- **Validates the Pre-Payroll** generated by the system: verifies that each line matches the approved [[Timesheet|Timesheets]] (ID, name, hours, rate, discounts, positions, hotel).
- **Configures and manages [[Deductions]]** per associate (uniform, food, 16% withholding).
- **Activates/deactivates the 16% withholding** when the associate submits documents (SSN/TaxID).
- **Manages internal rates**: when the associate's actual pay rate differs from the hotel's contractual rate, configures it in the system.
- **Executes the [[Vacation Pay]] calculation**: requests the system to calculate the average hours for the last 52 weeks with a breakdown by rate/hotel/position.
- **Reconciles** the check provider's response with the Pre-Payroll submitted.
