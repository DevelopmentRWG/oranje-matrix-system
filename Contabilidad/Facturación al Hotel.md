# Hotel Billing

Fiscal document automatically generated by the system at the close of the weekly period, detailing the charge to each hotel for the staffing services provided by Oranje.

## Generation

- The system generates the Invoice automatically at the end of the week (step 4 of the [[Contabilidad/Flujo de Nómina|Flujo de Nómina]])
- One invoice is generated **per hotel per week**
- The [[Manager de Contabilidad]] approves the invoice before sending it to the hotel

## Calculation

- **Regular hours** × bill rate from the [[Core/Módulos/Contrato|Contrato]]
- **Authorized overtime** × overtime rate from the Contract
- **Credits** (if applicable): the amount of the associate's [[Deducciones#Comida|food deduction]] is credited to the hotel

> [!important] The Hotel Invoice uses exclusively the **bill rate** from the Contract. The internal rate (what Oranje actually pays the associate) is never reflected in the Invoice.

## Special Rules

### Invoice Split by Month Crossover

- When a week spans two calendar months, the system generates **two separate invoices**: one for the days of the ending month and one for the days of the new month
- The [[Core/Módulos/Contrato|Contrato]] indicates whether the hotel requires this behavior (field: "Split invoice by month: yes/no")

### Authorized Overtime

- Only the overtime that the hotel **authorized** is billed to the hotel
- If the associate worked unauthorized overtime hours, those hours are not included in the Hotel Invoice (but may be paid to the associate at Oranje's discretion)

### Folio

- The invoice folio is generated by the Oranje system
- It is independent of any external reference

## Structure

| Field | Description |
| ----- | ----------- |
| Hotel | Hotel name and details |
| Period | Week date range (or fraction if split by month) |
| Detail per associate | Name, position, regular hours, authorized OT hours, rate, subtotal |
| Credits | Amounts in the hotel's favor (e.g. food deduction) |
| Total to charge | Sum of subtotals minus credits |
| Folio | Unique identifier generated by Oranje |
