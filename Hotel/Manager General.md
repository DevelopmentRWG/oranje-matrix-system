# General Manager

Role of highest authority within the hotel. Supervises the [[Hotel/Area Manager|Area Managers]] of the hotel's operational departments. The General Manager **always exists** in both hierarchies (see [[Hotel/Hotel|Hotel]]).

> [!note] Simple Hierarchy
> In hotels with a simple hierarchy, the General Manager also operates as [[Hotel/Area Manager|Area Manager]] (same person, two roles). In an extended hierarchy, they are different people.

## Responsibilities

### Requisitions

- Creates staff [[Requisition|requisitions]].
- **Approves** requisitions (status **Authorized**).
- **Rejects** requisitions, returning them to the creator with comments (status **In Progress**).

### Assigned Staff Management

- Generates the **QR** code for associates to punch in the [[Timesheet]].
- Places associates on rest (status **Pink — Stand-by** in the [[Associate Status Indicator]]).
- Reports associates (status **Red — Reported** in the [[Associate Status Indicator]]).
- Reports [[Core/Modules/Work Accident/Work Accident|workplace accidents]] detected on the property.

### General Oversight

- Global visibility of the [[Core/Modules/Schedule|Schedule]] and [[Timesheet]] across all [[Hotel Departments|departments]].
- Primary point of contact with Oranje at the management level.
