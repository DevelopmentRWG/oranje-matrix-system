# Recruiter

Operational role of the [[Recruitment/Recruitment|Recruitment]] module. Executes the process of searching, interviewing, validating and assigning collaborators based on a [[Requisition|Requisition]].

## Responsibilities

- Takes [[Requisition|requisitions]] from the shared inbox, prioritized by the [[Core/Modules/Status Lights/Requisition Urgency Status Light|Urgency Status Light]].
- Searches the [[Collaborator Pool|pool]] for available collaborators that meet the requisition.
- Checks the [[Core/Modules/Blacklist|Blacklist]] when a candidate applies, to avoid re-recruiting someone who has been banned.
- Conducts the initial interview and captures the collaborator's basic information:
  - Age
  - Gender
  - Full name
  - Address
  - Phone
- Validates and approves the collaborator after they complete their sign-up in the app.
- Enables the collaborator's access to the panels.
- Checks the hotel's [[Core/Modules/Schedule|Schedule]] to see the positions pending coverage.
- Assigns the collaborator to the hotel and registers them in its [[Core/Modules/Schedule|Schedule]].
- Temporarily assigns available collaborators (yellow → brown transition in the [[Collaborator Status Light|Collaborator Status Light]]).
