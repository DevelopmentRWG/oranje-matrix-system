# Recruitment Flow

Process through which [[Recruitment|Recruitment]] **sources, validates and onboards new collaborators** to feed the [[Collaborator Pool|Collaborator Pool]]. This flow does **not** cover requisitions directly: its goal is for the pool to have available collaborators so the [[Requisition Flow|Requisition Flow]] can consume them.

> [!info] Meeting point with the [[Requisition Flow|Requisition Flow]]
> The [[Collaborator Pool|Collaborator Pool]] is the only point where both flows connect. The Recruitment Flow **feeds** the pool; the [[Requisition Flow|Requisition Flow]] **consumes** it.

## Nature of the flow

The Recruitment Flow is **continuous**: [[Recruitment|Recruitment]] is **always hiring**, whether or not there are open requisitions. The goal is to keep the [[Collaborator Pool|Collaborator Pool]] healthy and ready for when the [[Requisition Flow|Requisition Flow]] needs to consume it.

It does not depend on an external trigger — it runs permanently. Requisitions without a match can **accelerate** or **prioritize** certain positions/zones, but they are not a condition to start the flow.

## Phases

### Phase 1 — Initial interview (recruitment panel)

Capture of the collaborator's initial information:
- Age
- Gender
- Full name
- Address
- Phone

### Phase 2 — Sign-up in the Oranje app (completed by the collaborator)

- SSN
- ITIN
- Position (see [[Posiciones|Positions]])
- English level (see [[English Levels|English Levels]])
- Experience level
- Type of transport
- Modality (see [[Employment Types|Hiring Modalities]])
- Proof of address (certificate of where they are living)

### Phase 3 — Validation and approval

[[Recruitment|Recruitment]] reviews the captured information and approves or rejects the collaborator.

### Phase 4 — Access enablement

The approved collaborator is granted access to the corresponding panels.

## Output

The approved collaborator **enters the [[Collaborator Pool|Collaborator Pool]]** with their [[Collaborator Status Light|Collaborator Status Light]] active. From there they become available to be assigned to a hotel through the [[Requisition Flow|Requisition Flow]].
