# Recruitment Manager

Role responsible for coordinating the intake of requisitions and supervising special cases within the [[Recruitment/Recruitment|Recruitment]] module.

## Responsibilities

- Monitors the shared [[Requisition|requisition]] inbox and intervenes manually only in exceptional cases: balancing between groups, absent leader and correction of an assignment error. Requisitions not taken for more than 24 hours are assigned automatically by the system.
- Reviews [[Core/Modules/Blacklist|Blacklist]] cases.
- Supervises the [[Recruiters Group Leader|Recruiters Group Leaders]].
- Has visibility of the **Extended Lunch Indicator** in the [[Timesheet|Timesheet]] to evaluate behavior patterns of active collaborators.
