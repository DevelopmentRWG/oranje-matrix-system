# Recruitment Manager

Role responsible for coordinating the intake of requisitions and supervising special cases within the [[Recruitment/Recruitment|Recruitment]] module.

## Responsibilities

- Monitors the shared [[Requisition|requisition]] inbox and intervenes manually only in exceptional cases: balancing between groups, absent leader and correction of an assignment error. Requisitions not taken for more than 24 hours are assigned automatically by the system.
- Reviews [[Core/Modules/Blacklist|Blacklist]] cases.
- Supervises the [[Recruiters Group Leader|Recruiters Group Leaders]].
- Has visibility of the **Extended Lunch Indicator** in the [[Timesheet|Timesheet]] to evaluate behavior patterns of active collaborators.
- Manages their direct team in the **My Team** module (RF-29): creates, edits, moves between groups and changes the status (including logical deactivation) of [[Recruiters Group Leader|Group Leaders]] and [[Recruiter|Recruiters]]. The only role in the department with full CRUD over the team.
- Consults the department **Global Dashboard** (RF-28): indicators for overall coverage, filled/partial/pending requisitions, open escalated cases and the [[Core/Modules/Status Lights/Quality Indicator|Quality Indicator]], with a trend view and a prioritized action inbox.
- Manages the department **Incidents** inbox (RF-30): receives cases escalated by Group Leaders or Inspectors, reviews the evidence and issues a final decision. The case lifecycle moves through the statuses Open → Under investigation → Awaiting info → Escalated to Commercial / Escalated to Management → Resolved. Incident types and SLAs are pending business validation.
- Can **resolve** an incident (RF-30) or **escalate it to Commercial** (RF-31), notifying the [[Sales/Roles/Business Developer|Business Developer]] or [[Sales/Roles/Business Developer Coordinator|Business Developer Coordinator]] assigned to the affected hotel so they can handle the commercial relationship.
- For critical cases that exceed their scope, can **escalate to Management**: the upward escalation path, separate from the follow-up reports generated for the department's own supervision.
- Audits the global history of candidate registrations and interviews across the department (RR-12): read-only access to all interviews recorded by any Recruiter or Group Leader; any direct intervention is exceptional and is logged in the audit trail.
