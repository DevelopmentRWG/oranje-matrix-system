# Recruiters Group Leader

Intermediate role of the [[Recruitment/Recruitment|Recruitment]] module. Executes the same operational functions as a [[Recruiter|Recruiter]] (recruitment and staff assignment) and also supervises the performance of an assigned group of Recruiters.

## Responsibilities

The Group Leader executes all the operational responsibilities of a [[Recruiter|Recruiter]], plus the following supervision functions:

- Executes all the operational responsibilities of a [[Recruiter|Recruiter]]: recruitment, interview, validation, approval and assignment of collaborators.
- Supervises and monitors the progress of the [[Recruiter|Recruiters]] in their group.
- Reports to the [[Recruitment Manager|Recruitment Manager]] the status and performance of their group.
