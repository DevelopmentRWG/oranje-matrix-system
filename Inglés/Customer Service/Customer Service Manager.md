# Customer Service Manager

Supervisory role of the [[Customer Service/Customer Service|Customer Service]] department. Responsible for coordinating the team of [[Customer Service/Customer Service Agent|agents]], managing escalations that exceed the agent's capacity, and reporting client hotel satisfaction metrics.

## Responsibilities

### Team supervision

- Supervises the performance of the [[Customer Service/Customer Service Agent|Customer Service Agents]].
- Assigns cases according to workload and specialty.
- Ensures compliance with the response times defined in the [[Customer Service/Customer Service Rules|Customer Service Business Rules]].

### Escalation management

- Receives cases escalated by agents that require higher-level intervention.
- Coordinates with the [[Sales/Roles/Business Developer Coordinator|Business Developer Coordinator]] for commercial or contractual matters.
- Coordinates with the [[Inspection/Coordinator|Inspection Coordinator]] for incidents that require on-site verification.
- Escalates to management the cases that cannot be resolved within the department.

### Metrics and reports

- Reports satisfaction metrics and resolution times.
- Collaborates with the [[QA Operator|QA Operator]] assigned to the department for the review of KPIs.
- Identifies recurring patterns in requests to propose operational improvements.
