# Customer Service

Module responsible for post-onboarding support to the active client hotel. Customer Service is the formal communication channel between the hotel and Oranje for inquiries, requests, complaints and follow-up of incidents that do not fall under the predefined operational flows ([[Requisition|requisitions]], [[Core/Modules/Schedule|Schedule]], [[Timesheet]]).

It operates from the moment the hotel reaches **Orange** status in the [[Core/Modules/Status Lights/Onboarding Status Light|Onboarding Status Light]] and remains the point of contact throughout the hotel's entire life as a client.

## Roles

- [[Customer Service/Customer Service Manager|Customer Service Manager]] — Supervises the team of agents, manages escalations and reports satisfaction metrics.
- [[Customer Service/Customer Service Agent|Customer Service Agent]] — Receives, documents and follows up on the hotel's requests until they are closed.

## Scope

### What Customer Service handles

- General hotel inquiries about operating with Oranje.
- Complaints or grievances about assigned collaborators, billing or processes.
- Requests that do not fit the existing operational flows.
- Follow-up of commercial disputes before they escalate to **Black** status in the [[Core/Modules/Status Lights/Onboarding Status Light|Onboarding Status Light]].
- Coordination with internal departments to resolve incidents reported by the hotel.

### What Customer Service does NOT handle

- Creation or authorization of [[Requisition|requisitions]] — that is the responsibility of the [[Hotel/Hotel|Hotel]].
- Staff assignment — that is the responsibility of [[Recruitment/Recruitment|Recruitment]].
- On-site verification of collaborators — that is the responsibility of [[Inspection/Inspection|Inspection]].
- Acquisition of new hotels — that is the responsibility of [[Sales/Sales|Sales]].

## Relationship with other modules

- [[Hotel/Hotel|Hotel]] — The hotel is the issuer of support requests. The [[Hotel/General Manager|General Manager]] is the main point of contact.
- [[Sales/Sales|Sales]] — Customer Service is the continuation of the commercial cycle post-conversion. The [[Sales/Roles/Business Developer Coordinator|BDC]] is the escalation point for commercial matters.
- [[QA/QA|QA]] — A [[QA Operator|QA Operator]] supervises the performance metrics of Customer Service.
- [[Inspection/Inspection|Inspection]] — For incidents that require on-site verification, Customer Service coordinates with the Inspection [[Inspection/Coordinator|Coordinator]].
