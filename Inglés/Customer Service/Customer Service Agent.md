# Customer Service Agent

Operational role of the [[Customer Service/Customer Service|Customer Service]] department. It is the first point of contact for requests from the client hotel. Receives, documents and follows up on each case until it is closed or escalated.

## Responsibilities

### Handling requests

- Receives requests from the hotel through the enabled channels.
- Documents each case with: date, hotel, contact, description and category.
- Provides an initial response within the times defined in the [[Customer Service/Customer Service Rules|Customer Service Business Rules]].

### Follow-up and closure

- Follows up on open cases until they are resolved.
- Coordinates with the relevant internal departments to obtain the necessary information or action.
- Documents the resolution and closes the case.

### Escalation

- Escalates to the [[Customer Service/Customer Service Manager|Customer Service Manager]] any cases that exceed its resolution capacity or the defined times.
- Immediately escalates any case that involves a possible commercial dispute or risk of transition to **Black** status in the [[Core/Modules/Status Lights/Onboarding Status Light|Onboarding Status Light]].
