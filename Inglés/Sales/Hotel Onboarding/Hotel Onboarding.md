# Hotel Onboarding

Module that represents the commercial phase prior to a hotel becoming an operational client of Oranje. It covers the cycle from when the [[Business Developer]] identifies a hotel in their territory until the [[Business Developer Coordinator]] approves the conversion and the hotel is enabled to generate [[Requisition|requisitions]].

> [!info]
> The hotel **only enables requisitions** when it reaches the [[Onboarding Status Light#Naranja — Acuerdo firmado, hotel cliente activo|Orange]] status. Before that it remains as a commercial prospect managed by [[Sales/Sales|Sales]].

## Module contents

### Roles

- [[Business Developer]] — Responsible for the commercial cycle with the hotel: contacts, proposes and follows up.
- [[Business Developer Coordinator]] — Supervises routes and zones, validates terms and gives the final yes to the conversion.

### Processes

- [[Onboarding Flow|Onboarding Flow]] — Step-by-step process from identification to activation.
- [[Onboarding Status Light|Onboarding Status Light]] — Tracking statuses of the negotiation with the hotel.

## Onboarding statuses

- [[Onboarding Status Light#Gris — Hotel identificado|Gray]] — Hotel identified.
- [[Onboarding Status Light#Azul Claro — Contacto y recopilación de datos|Light Blue]] — Contact and data collection.
- [[Onboarding Status Light#Verde — Propuesta enviada|Green]] — Proposal sent.
- [[Onboarding Status Light#Amarillo — En seguimiento tras propuesta|Yellow]] — Follow-up after proposal.
- [[Onboarding Status Light#Rosa — Negociación de términos|Pink]] — Terms negotiation.
- [[Onboarding Status Light#Naranja — Acuerdo firmado, hotel cliente activo|Orange]] — Agreement signed, active client hotel.
- [[Onboarding Status Light#Café — Renegociación / desbloqueo|Brown]] — Renegotiation / unblocking.
- [[Onboarding Status Light#Rojo — Rechazo o no interés|Red]] — Rejection or no interest.
- [[Onboarding Status Light#Negro — Cliente pausado o inactivo|Black]] — Client paused or inactive.
