# Hotel User

Account of the hotel in the Oranje system. It is created upon approving the conversion in [[Onboarding Status Light|Onboarding Status Pink]], just before the [[Automatic Conversion Trigger|Automatic Conversion Trigger]], and marks the transition of the hotel from commercial prospect to **active client** in [[Onboarding Status Light|Onboarding Status Orange]].

## Creation

- **Responsible for the approval:** [[Business Developer Coordinator]].
- **Moment:** upon closing the agreement in [[Onboarding Status Light|Onboarding Status Pink]].
- **Immediate effect:** fires the [[Automatic Conversion Trigger|Automatic Conversion Trigger]].

## What it enables

- Hotel access to the system as a client.
- Ability to generate [[Requisition|requisitions]] from the [[Hotel/Hotel|Hotel]] module.
- Management of the weekly [[Core/Modules/Schedule|Schedule]], from where the requisitions, assignments and the [[Timesheet]] of its collaborators are administered.
