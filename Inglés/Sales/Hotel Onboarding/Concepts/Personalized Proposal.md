# Customized Proposal

Commercial document that the [[Business Developer]] prepares and sends to the hotel during [[Onboarding Status Light|Onboarding Status Green]]. It presents Oranje's offer adapted to the specific needs of the prospect hotel.

## Content

- Services offered.
- Tentative prices (pay rate / bill rate).
- General conditions of the commercial relationship.

## Lifecycle

- **Prepared and sent in:** [[Onboarding Status Light|Onboarding Status Green]].
- **Followed up in:** [[Onboarding Status Light|Onboarding Status Green]] (record of contact attempts).
- **Adjusted/resumed from:** [[Onboarding Status Light|Onboarding Status Brown]] when there is a stall.
