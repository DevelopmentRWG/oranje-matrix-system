# Business Developer

Operational role of the [[Hotel Onboarding|Hotel Onboarding]] module. Responsible for the commercial cycle with the hotel: in charge of contacting, presenting, proposing and following up with the hotel while the negotiation between Oranje and the hotel is taking place.

## Responsibilities

- Identifies prospect hotels within their assigned territory.
- Creates the hotel profile in the system and collects initial data.
- Performs a cold visit to the hotel.
- Prepares and sends the [[Personalized Proposal|Customized Proposal]] to the hotel.
- Records contact attempts and responses.
- Provides continuous follow-up during the negotiation.
- Participates in the terms negotiation together with the [[Business Developer Coordinator]].
- Remains as commercial reference once the hotel is active.

## Statuses in which it participates

- [[Onboarding Status Light#Gris — Hotel identificado|Gray]] — Prospect identification.
- [[Onboarding Status Light#Azul Claro — Contacto y recopilación de datos|Light Blue]] — Contact and data collection.
- [[Onboarding Status Light#Verde — Propuesta enviada|Green]] — Proposal sent.
- [[Onboarding Status Light#Amarillo — En seguimiento tras propuesta|Yellow]] — Follow-up after proposal.
- [[Onboarding Status Light#Rosa — Negociación de términos|Pink]] — Terms negotiation (together with BDC).
- [[Onboarding Status Light#Rojo — Rechazo o no interés|Red]] — Rejection management and possible reactivation.
