# Business Developer Coordinator

Supervisory role of the [[Hotel Onboarding|Hotel Onboarding]] module. Supervises the entire panorama of the routes and zones assigned to them, follows up with the [[Business Developer]] in their negotiation with the hotel, validates the terms and conditions before closing the [[Contrato|Contract]]; is the **final yes** of the negotiation.

## Responsibilities

- Supervises the BDs in their assigned territory and routes.
- Intervenes in cases of stall or renegotiation.
- Investigates and provides a solution to resume stalled proposals.
- Validates the terms of the [[Terms and Conditions Document|Terms and Conditions Document]].
- Approves the conversion of the hotel in [[Onboarding Status Light#Rosa — Negociación de términos|Pink]], triggering the creation of the [[Hotel User|Hotel User]] and the [[Automatic Conversion Trigger|Automatic Conversion Trigger]].
- Manages paused relationships or clients that stop operating.

## Statuses in which it participates

- [[Onboarding Status Light#Café — Renegociación / desbloqueo|Brown]] — Renegotiation / unblocking.
- [[Onboarding Status Light#Amarillo — En seguimiento tras propuesta|Yellow]] — Supports the creation of the T&C document.
- [[Onboarding Status Light#Rosa — Negociación de términos|Pink]] — Approves the conversion (final yes).
- [[Onboarding Status Light#Naranja — Acuerdo firmado, hotel cliente activo|Orange]] — Commercial reference of the active hotel.
- [[Onboarding Status Light#Negro — Cliente pausado o inactivo|Black]] — Management of the paused client and possible reactivation.
