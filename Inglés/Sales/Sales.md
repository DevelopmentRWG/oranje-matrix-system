# Sales

Module responsible for the incorporation of new hotels into the Oranje platform (onboarding) and the commercial management with clients.

## Roles

- [[Business Developer]] — Commercial cycle with the hotel: contacts, proposes and follows up.
- [[Business Developer Coordinator]] — Supervises routes and zones, validates terms and approves the conversion.

## Processes

- [[Hotel Onboarding|Hotel Onboarding]] — Commercial phase from the identification of a hotel to its activation as a client.
- [[Onboarding Flow|Onboarding Flow]] — Step-by-step onboarding process.
- [[Onboarding Status Light|Onboarding Status Light]] — Tracking statuses of the negotiation with the hotel.
