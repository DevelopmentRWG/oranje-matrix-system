# Accountant

Operational role in charge of executing the weekly financial processes within the Oranje system: review of the Weekly Summary, validation of the Pre-Payroll, management of deductions, configuration of internal rates, reconciliation with the check provider and calculation of vacation pay.

## Responsibilities

- **Reviews the [[Collaborator Weekly Summary|Collaborator Weekly Summary]]**: verifies it is in order before sending it for the [[Accounting Manager|Accounting Manager]]'s approval.
- **Validates the Pre-Payroll** generated by the system: verifies that each line matches the approved [[Timesheet|Timesheets]] (ID, name, hours, rate, deductions, positions, hotel).
- **Configures and manages [[Deductions|Deductions]]** per collaborator (uniform, meal, 16% withholding).
- **Activates/deactivates the 16% withholding** when the collaborator submits documents (SSN/TaxID).
- **Manages internal rates**: when the collaborator's actual pay rate differs from the hotel's contractual rate, configures it in the system.
- **Executes the [[Vacation|Vacation]] calculation**: requests from the system the average hours over the last 52 weeks with a breakdown by rate/hotel/position.
- **Reconciles** the check provider's response with the submitted Pre-Payroll.
