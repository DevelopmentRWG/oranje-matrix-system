# Accounting Manager

Supervisory role in charge of approving and authorizing the weekly financial processes within the Oranje system: approval of the Weekly Summary, authorization of the payroll and approval of the hotel invoicing.

## Responsibilities

- **Approves the [[Collaborator Weekly Summary|Collaborator Weekly Summary]]** before releasing the payment to the collaborator (after the [[Accountant|Accountant]] reviews it).
- **Authorizes partial overtime**: when the hotel does not approve all the overtime worked, decides the billable/payable OT hours according to what was authorized.
- **Approves the [[Hotel Invoicing|Hotel Invoice]]**: validates that the automatically generated invoice correctly reflects the bill rate and the authorized hours.
- **Authorizes the final payroll** to release the payments.
- **Supervises and coordinates** the [[Accountant|Accountant]]'s work.
