# Hotel Invoicing

Tax document generated automatically by the system at the close of the weekly period, detailing the charge to each hotel for the staffing services provided by Oranje.

## Generation

- The system generates the Invoice automatically at the end of the week (step 4 of the [[Accounting/Payroll Flow|Payroll Flow]])
- One invoice is generated **per hotel per week**
- The [[Accounting Manager|Accounting Manager]] approves the invoice before sending it to the hotel

## Calculation

- **Regular hours** × bill rate from the [[Core/Modules/Contrato|Contract]]
- **Authorized overtime** × the Contract's overtime rate
- **Credits** (if applicable): the amount of the collaborator's [[Deductions#Comida|meal deduction]] is credited to the hotel

> [!important] The Hotel Invoice uses exclusively the **bill rate** from the Contract. The internal rate (what Oranje actually pays the collaborator) is never reflected in the Invoice.

## Special rules

### Invoice split by month crossover

- When a week spans two calendar months, the system generates **two separate invoices**: one for the days of the ending month and another for the days of the new month
- The [[Core/Modules/Contrato|Contract]] indicates whether the hotel requires this behavior (field: "Invoice split by month: yes/no")

### Authorized overtime

- Only the overtime the hotel **authorized** is billed to the hotel
- If the collaborator worked unauthorized overtime, those hours are not included in the Hotel Invoice (but they may still be paid to the collaborator at Oranje's discretion)

### Folio

- The invoice folio is generated by the Oranje system
- It is independent of any external reference

## Structure

| Field | Description |
| ----- | ----------- |
| Hotel | Hotel name and details |
| Period | Date range of the week (or fraction if there is a month split) |
| Detail per collaborator | Name, position, regular hours, authorized OT hours, rate, subtotal |
| Credits | Amounts in the hotel's favor (e.g. meal deduction) |
| Total to charge | Sum of subtotals minus credits |
| Folio | Unique identifier generated by Oranje |
