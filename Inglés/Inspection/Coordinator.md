# Coordinator

Supervisory role of the Inspection department. He is the head of the [[Inspector|inspectors]], responsible for zone assignment, the review of their field work and the general coordination of the inspection operation.

## Responsibilities

- Assigns [[Inspector|inspectors]] to the geographic [[Zones|zones]].
- Reviews and supervises the inspectors' work in the field.
- Coordinates the general operation of the Inspection department.
- Escalates problems or cases that require special attention.
- Liaison between the Inspection department and other departments ([[Recruitment/Recruitment|Recruitment]], [[Hotel/Hotel|Hotel]]).
- Has visibility of the **Extended Lunch Indicator** in the [[Timesheet]] to supervise extended lunch patterns in the assigned zones.
- When an [[Inspector]] is not available (illness, vacation or another cause), temporarily reassigns another Inspector to that zone to guarantee operational coverage.
