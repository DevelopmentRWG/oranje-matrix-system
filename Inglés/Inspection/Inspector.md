# Inspector

Operational role in charge of the on-site supervision of the collaborators assigned to a hotel, of arrival verification, of uniform delivery and of the investigation of reported cases.

## Responsibilities

- **Verifies the collaborator's arrival on day 1** at the property (`Strong Green → Apple Green` transition in the [[Collaborator Status Light|Collaborator Status Light]]).
- **Delivers the uniform** to the collaborator when they transition to `Light Blue` (Day 3+) in the [[Collaborator Status Light|Collaborator Status Light]].
- Supervises on site the operation of the collaborators in the hotel.
- Investigates the cases of collaborators in `Red` state (reported by the [[Hotel/Area Manager|Area Manager]]) in the [[Collaborator Status Light|Collaborator Status Light]].
- **Has their own authority to decide** the result of the investigation, without needing escalation:
  - `Black` ([[Core/Modules/Blacklist|Blacklist]]) — if the dispute is resolved in favor of the hotel.
  - `Strong Green` (reinstated) — if the dispute is resolved in favor of the collaborator.
- Supports the operation of the active client hotel ([[Onboarding Status Light#Naranja — Acuerdo firmado, hotel cliente activo|Onboarding Orange]]).
- Receives notification of a [[Core/Modules/Workplace Accident/Workplace Accident|workplace accident]] in their zone (see [[Core/Modules/Workplace Accident/Workplace Accident Flow|Workplace Accident Flow]]).
- Completes the accident card with medical follow-up information: transfer, diagnosis, days of disability, observations.
- Is the **final party responsible for closing** the accident card, once the information is complete.
- Upon closure, manages the collaborator's `Gray → Strong Green` transition in the [[Collaborator Status Light|Collaborator Status Light]] when they receive medical discharge.
- Consults the **Extended Lunch Indicator** in the [[Timesheet]] to identify collaborators whose lunch exceeds 30 minutes in their zone.
