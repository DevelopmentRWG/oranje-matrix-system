## Description

Role with **maximum authority on the hotel side**. **Always exists**, in both simple and extended hierarchy.

- **In simple hierarchy:** the General Manager also operates as Area Manager (same person, two roles). Executes all operational actions: creates/approves requisitions, manages Schedule and Timesheet, generates QR, reports collaborators and accidents.
- **In extended hierarchy:** supervises the Area Managers (one per department: Housekeeping, Food, Maintenance, Front Desk). Keeps global visibility of the hotel and can also operate (authorize requisitions, generate QR, etc.) when intervening on specific occasions.

---

## Objective in the System

Ensure the correct operational functioning of the hotel: validate requisitions, supervise the departments, execute operational actions when applicable, escalate problems and report to direction.

---

## Main Actions

### Operational (shared with Area Manager)
- Create requisitions
- Authorize / reject requisitions
- Generate / renew Timesheet QR
- Edit weekly Schedule
- Correct punches
- Put collaborator in Stand-by (Pink)
- Report collaborator (Red)
- Report workplace accidents

### Executive (exclusive to the General Manager)
- Global visibility of the Schedule and Timesheet of all departments
- Supervise Area Managers and Supervisors
- Comment on requisition files
- Escalate delayed requisitions to Recruitment
- Request reports from Area Managers
- Generate executive reports
- Send reports to direction
- Schedule recurring sends
