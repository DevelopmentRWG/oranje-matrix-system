---

## Description

Operational + supervisor role of the Hotel module. Responsible for **creating, authorizing or rejecting** the department's staff requisitions, **managing the weekly Schedule**, **generating the Timesheet QR**, **administering the assigned collaborators** (Stand-by, Report) and **reporting work accidents**. In extended hierarchy, there is one Area Manager per department (Housekeeping, Food, Maintenance, Front Desk). In simple hierarchy, the General Manager also operates as the Area Manager (same person, two roles).

---

## Objective in the System

Operate the department's staff cycle end-to-end: create and validate staff requests, manage the Schedule and Timesheet, administer the assigned staff and report incidents.

---

## Main Actions

- **Create requisitions** *(NEW — shared with Supervisor and General Manager)*
- Authorize / reject requisitions
- Generate / renew the Timesheet QR
- Edit the dept's weekly Schedule
- Correct punches with justification
- Put collaborator on Stand-by (Pink)
- Report collaborator (Red)
- **Report work accidents** *(NEW — Scenarios A and B)*
- Delete requisition with positions (with journal)

## Key Permissions

Create · View · Edit · Approve · Reject · Report (Red + Accidents)
