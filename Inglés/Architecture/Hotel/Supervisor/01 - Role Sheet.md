---

## Description

Operational role of the Hotel module. Subordinate to the Area Manager / Department Manager. Their main task is to **create the staffing requisitions** the hotel needs and send them to the Area Manager for authorization. They also **report workplace accidents** detected on the property and consult the department Schedule and Timesheet.

---

## Objective in the System

Capture the hotel's operational staffing need and formalize it through requisitions; report workplace accidents with on-site detail; consult the department's operation.

---

## Main Actions

- Create staffing requisitions
- Edit requisition drafts
- Send requisition for authorization
- Suggest staffing reinforcement (prefill from Schedule)
- Report workplace accidents (Scenarios A and B)
- Put collaborator on Stand-by (Pink) — shared with Area Manager and General Manager
- **Report collaborator (Red)** *(NEW — shared with Area Manager and General Manager)*
- Consult department Schedule and Timesheet

---

## Key Permissions

Create · View · Edit (no deletion except drafts) · Report (Red + Accidents)
