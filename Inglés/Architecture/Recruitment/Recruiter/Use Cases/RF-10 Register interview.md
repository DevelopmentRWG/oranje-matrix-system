# 🪪 ID: RF-10
🏷️ **Name:** Record interview

**Story:**
The Recruiter documents the outcome of the interview conducted with the candidate (approved, rejected, observations). The record is linked to the candidate's profile with date and author for traceability.

**Acceptance criteria:**
The record is linked to the candidate's profile. Visible in the interview history. Includes date, author (Recruiter), result and observations.

**Documentation:**
- PRD: PRD-RECL-02 Recruiter
- Flow: Interview record
- Prototype: (Figma link)

**Flow:**
`Candidate profile` → MANUAL → `Click "Record interview"` → `Fill out form (result, observations)` → `Confirm` → AUTOMATIC → `Records in history + Links to candidate profile`
