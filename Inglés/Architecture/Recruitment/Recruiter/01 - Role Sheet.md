## Description

Person in the recruitment area who executes searching, interviewing, validating, and assigning collaborators. Freely takes requisitions from the Authorized inbox (Self-Pick model) and covers them with candidates from the Pool.

---

## Objective in the System

To attract and register collaborators that feed the Collaborator Pool, and to assign them to hotels according to the requisitions she decides to take from the inbox.

---

## Main Actions

- Take requisition from the inbox (Self-Pick)
- Interview candidates (Phase 1)
- Validate sign-up in App (Phase 2)
- Enable the collaborator's access
- Check Blacklist
- Assign collaborator to hotel/Schedule
- Mark requisition as covered or partial
