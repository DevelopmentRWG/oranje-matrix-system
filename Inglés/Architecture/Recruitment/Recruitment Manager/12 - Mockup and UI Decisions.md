# Mockup and UI Decisions — Recruitment Manager

Documents the interactive web mockup for the Recruitment Manager role and the design decisions made during its implementation.

> [!info]
> The mockup lives in the mockups repo: `oranje-mockups/Mockups/Reclutamiento/Manager de reclutamiento/Manager de Reclutamiento - Oranje.html`. The visual source of truth (tokens and components) is `oranje-seed.css` — see [[Design Conventions]].

---

## Implemented Screens

| Screen | Description |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Department-wide overview: global coverage hero, 4 KPIs (coverage, covered, open escalated cases, quality indicator), a **coverage trend chart** (last 6 weeks), a **requisitions-by-urgency breakdown** (each bar drills down to filtered Requisitions), an **Interviews pipeline** (each stage links to that tray in Recruitment), an **action tray** with 3 tabs (Critical requisitions · Escalations · Team), Leaders ranking, and coverage by zone. Clicking a zone **drills down** to Requisitions filtered by that zone. Supervision view + navigation. RF-27, RF-28. |
| **Requisitions** | Same design as the Group Leader. Department-wide view with tabs All / Critical-Stranded / My taken, chip-dropdown **filters** (urgency, position, zone, Leader, search by ID/hotel) and **3 switchable views** — Board (kanban by urgency: Urgent / Soon / Normal) · Cards · Table — with rich cards (per-position coverage status light, contract/modality, period). Detail drawer with timeline (History, RR-16) and participants (collaborative model, RR-15). Four **exceptional actions** by the Manager, each requiring a mandatory justification: **Take as Manager** (RF-EXC-01), **Assign to Recruiter** (RF-EXC-02), **Reassign** (RF-EXC-03), and **Force status flag** (RF-21). |
| **Recruitment** | Same design as the Group Leader. Two sub-views (Collaborator Pool / Interviews). **Pool:** hero with stats + **4 switchable views** — Board (kanban by [[Collaborator Status Light]] state) · Cards · Table · By zone —, chip filters (Position, Zone, Modality, English, Leader, Status) + search, rich cards (avatar with status ring, position/zone/modality, English level, history) and a collaborator drawer with tabs (Data / Work / History / Documents). **New collaborator** sign-up (exceptional support). **Interviews:** hero with KPIs, Department-wide history / My interviews toggle, filters (Position, Zone, Modality, **Origin**, Status, **Days in status**, Leader, Recruiter), a **kanban board** by status (Pending app / Pending validation / Abandoned / Validated 30 days) with Board/Cards/Table views and a per-status CTA (Send reminder / Validate / Reactivate / View history), and a **file drawer** with a phase stepper (P1 interview → P2 data → P3 documents → Validation RF-08), recruiter traceability, candidate data, and a status panel; supervision actions (view performance RF-23, **internal note** to the audit log, **request action** —a structured request that notifies, no chat—, reassign, validate/reject on intervention, mark abandoned). Global scope; operational actions are **exceptional support** and recorded in the audit log (RR-12). |
| **Blacklist** | Same design as the Group Leader (reused `blacklist.js` module). Dark hero + KPIs (total banned, for 3 absences, serious misconduct) and **3 switchable views** — Board (kanban by reason: 3 absences / Serious misconduct) · Cards · Table —, with **filters** (Zone · Reason · **Banned by** · **Period** with a range calendar) + search. **5-tab drawer** (Data / Work / History / Documents / Blacklist) with reason, evidence and an entry timeline, all **read-only**. Adding via a **multi-step modal** (4 steps: pool search with filters + "View profile", type of serious misconduct, justification, evidence). Any department role can **Add** with reason and evidence (RR-03). **Note: the ban (Black status) is permanent — there is no removal or rehabilitation. Disputes are resolved by the Zone Inspector (Red → Black/reinstatement), not the Manager.** |
| **My Team** | **Manager-exclusive.** Hero with 4 stats (active Leaders, Recruiters, average coverage, escalated cases) + an **accordion of cards per Group Leader** (gradient avatar, status pill, zone, number of Recruiters, escalations, and a coverage bar) that expands to show their **nested Recruiters** as sub-cards (coverage, assignment time, escalations) with Metrics/Move actions and an Edit Leader / Change status footer. Individual metrics per Recruiter regardless of her Leader (RF-23). Management (RF-29): create Leader / Recruiter, edit, move between groups, and change status (active / vacation / termination). With **validations**: blocks duplicate email on creation, and prevents terminating a Leader who still has active Recruiters without reassigning their group first. |
| **Incidents** | **Manager-exclusive.** Tray of cases escalated from Leaders, Inspectors, and the system. Hero with clickable KPIs (open, under investigation, escalated to sales, SLA at risk), chip **filters** (Priority, Status, Origin, Zone) + search and **2 views** — **Board** (kanban by the case lifecycle: Open → Under investigation → Awaiting info → Escalated to sales → Escalated to Management) · Table. **Case file** with 4 tabs (Detail with description and a state banner / **Evidence** with files / **Involved parties** —Leader, Recruiter, Inspector, hotel— / **History** timeline). RF-30 flow: the case starts **Open** and the **Investigate** button moves it to *Under investigation* before deciding; final decision **Resolve** (RF-30, mandatory comment → closes and notifies the parties), **Escalate to sales** (RF-31 → hotel BD/BDC), or **Escalate to Management** (requires a summary). Transitions move the case across columns and update the KPIs and sidebar counter live; every decision is recorded in the audit log. |
| **Reports** | Same department visual language. Hero with 4 stats (received this week, unreviewed, scheduled sends, global coverage). **Reports received from Leaders**: rich list with avatar, group, coverage and read/unread state; the detail opens in a **drawer** (group indicators + Download / Comment to the Leader, and marks the report as read). **Generate global report** (RF-24): report type, date range, **filters by zone and by Leader** (multi-select), format (PDF / Excel / CSV) and recipient (for the Manager's own tracking and supervision); **preview** and **schedule recurring send**. A **Scheduled sends (recurring)** section that can be added to (frequency/when/format/recipient modal) and removed from. Fields in [[09 - Form Fields]]. Note: reporting to Management is QA's responsibility, not the Recruitment Manager's. |

> [!note]
> The **global search**, **Notifications** (bell), and **My Profile** (avatar) live in the header, not the sidebar. They are not independent modules. The profile menu also includes the **ES/EN language toggle**.

## UI Decisions

- **Supervisor who intervenes, not a distributor.** The Manager does **not** hand out requisitions: the normal flow is Self-Pick ([[Requisition Self-Pick]]), where Recruiters and Leaders take them freely. The mockup exposes only **exceptional intervention** (take / assign / reassign / force status flag), never routine distribution. Rationale: RR-15.
- **Every manual intervention requires justification → audit log.** Each exceptional action (take, assign, reassign, force status flag, add to Blacklist) **validates a mandatory reason** before executing and shows that it is recorded in the log. Rationale: RR-12.
- **No chat or user-to-user messaging.** The platform **has no chat**; the mockup exposes no "comment / message to a person" buttons. Cross-role communication is modeled with **notifications** (one-way), **escalations**, **structured requests** that trigger a notification, and **justification comments recorded in the audit log** (e.g. when resolving an incident RF-30 or adding to the Blacklist RF-12). Accordingly, "Comment to the Leader" (Reports) was replaced with navigation to My Team, and "comment on file" (Interviews) was reframed as an **internal note to the log**.
- **No "Released" status (collaborative model).** The requisition uses the **join / leave** model: several recruiters participate at once, joining does not transfer or block, and coverage progress is shared. The drawer shows participants and the model banner; there is no "release" action. Rationale: RR-15.
- **Blacklist: global consult and add.** The Manager can consult the full department blacklist and add collaborators, the same as any Recruitment role. The ban (Black status) is **permanent**: there is no removal or rehabilitation. Disputes (Red status of the collaborator) are resolved by the **Zone Inspector** (Red → Black/reinstatement) — not the Manager. Rationale: RR-03 (RF-12).
- **My Team and Incidents as exclusive modules.** They reflect the two functions no other department role has: management and supervision of Leaders and Recruiters (RF-29, RF-23), and intake + final decision on escalations (RF-30, RF-31).
- **Recruitment is a supervisor's global view.** The Manager sees the entire department pool; operational actions appear as exceptional support to emphasize that the role is to audit and supervise, not to recruit daily.
- **Interviews sub-view to audit the department.** The Manager's main use of Recruitment is to supervise all candidate sign-ups: the file is read-only for them (managed by the Recruiter) and their actions (validate/reject on intervention, reassign, mark abandoned, request action) are exceptional and recorded in the audit log. The days-in-status indicator highlights stuck candidates. Rationale: RR-12; Interviews sub-view of [[00 - Manager Architecture]].
- **Search, notifications, and profile in the header.** Following the platform's global convention. See [[General App Structure]].
- **Shared design seed.** The mockup links `oranje-seed.css` and only adds Manager-specific styles (side drawer, action tray, team sub-rows, priority pills). It does not reimplement components. See [[Design Conventions]].
- **Design consistency with the Group Leader (shared modules).** Requisitions, Recruitment (Pool + Interviews), and Blacklist reuse the **same design system as the Group Leader** — the department's reference role: a *hero* with stat cards, full-width tabs with counts, *chip-dropdown* filters + search, rich tables with per-position status lights, and same-style drawers (`recl-*`, `req-table-rich`, `bl-*`, `t-*`). The mockup loads the department component sheet (`recl-components.css`, taken from the Leader) + `schedule-blacklist.css` **above** the seed; the only differences from the Leader are the **global scope** (all zones/Leaders) and the **Manager-exclusive actions**. Dashboard is a Manager-specific module on the seed; it includes a trend chart, urgency breakdown, and Interviews pipeline.
- **Single identity extended to the exclusive modules.** Although **My Team**, **Incidents**, and **Reports** are Manager-specific (they don't exist in the Leader), they adopt the **same visual language** as the shared modules —a *hero* with stat cards, *chip-dropdown* filters + search, rich tables/boards, and `recl-*`/`req-table-rich` drawers— instead of their own look, so the whole platform reads as one product. My Team expresses it as a Leader accordion; Incidents as a tray with a rich table + kanban board; Reports as an inbox of received reports + a generator with preview and recurring sends. Rationale: one coherent visual identity (see [[Design Conventions]]).
- **Incidents as an operational tray, not just a list.** The module models the case **lifecycle** as a kanban board (Open → Under investigation → Awaiting info → Escalated to sales → Escalated to Management) and requires the **Investigate** step before **Resolve** (RF-30 flow); decisions move the case across columns and update the KPIs and sidebar counter live. The **case file** gathers Detail, Evidence, **Involved parties** (Leader, Recruiter, Inspector, hotel), and History (timeline). *Decisions taken in the face of documentation gaps, now formalized:* the lifecycle states are formalized in [[13 - Incident States]] (inferred from RF-30/RF-31; the only state explicitly named in the RFs is "Escalated to sales" — the remaining names come from the mockup and are subject to validation); SLA and timing rules are formalized in [[14 - Incident SLA]] (proposal pending business validation); the incident case fields and the resolution and escalation-to-sales forms are formalized in [[09 - Form Fields]]. The mockup represents priority as a **visual affordance** (not a documented field) and **does not invent** an SLA countdown, days-in-status, or assign/reassign (none documented).
- **No decorative UI: everything that looks clickable works.** Stats that filter, *chip-dropdowns*, view switchers (Board / Cards / Table), cards that open their drawer, state transitions, and modal actions are all wired; there are no inert buttons or filler data.
- **Drawer mounting (technical note).** Modules that reuse the Leader's case file (Requisitions, Recruitment Pool, Incidents) mount its `.req-drawer` content **inside the Manager's own side panel** (`.drawer`). Since `.req-drawer` carries its own fixed positioning, a `.drawer > .req-drawer` rule neutralizes it so the file fills the panel; without it the drawer rendered off-screen.
- **Bilingual ES/EN.** i18n engine (exact dictionary + patterns + `MutationObserver`) with an **ES/EN toggle** in the profile menu; it translates navigation, modules, drawers, modals, dropdowns, and toasts. Proper nouns (people, hotels) are preserved.
- **Device: Web – Desktop.** The Manager operates from a web desktop; the mockup leverages that density (tables, side drawers, multi-panel views).
