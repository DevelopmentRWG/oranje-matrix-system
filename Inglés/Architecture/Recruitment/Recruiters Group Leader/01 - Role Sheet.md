# ROLE SHEET — RECRUITERS GROUP LEADER

---

| Field             | Content                              |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Role ID**       | ROL-02                               |
| **Role Name**     | 🧑‍🏫 Recruiters Group Leader         |
| **Type**          | Intermediate                         |
| **Reports to**    | Recruitment Manager                  |
| **Supervises**    | Recruiters in their group            |

---

## Description

Intermediate role that executes all the operational functions of a Recruiter and additionally supervises the performance of a group of Recruiters under their charge. Freely takes requisitions from the inbox (Self-Pick) and reports the group's status and performance to the Manager.

---

## System Objective

Cover requisitions like any Recruiter + supervise the performance of the group under their charge and report metrics to the Manager.

---

## Main Actions

- Take requisition from the inbox (Self-Pick)
- Execute all the operational functions of a Recruiter (interview, validate, assign)
- Supervise the group's Recruiters
- Generate and send reports to the Manager
- Handle first-level incidents of the group

---

Create · View · Edit · Assign · Report
