# 🪪 ID: RF-C-03
🏷️ **Name:** Clock in via QR

**Story:**
The Collaborator arrives at the hotel and needs to record their attendance by scanning the QR code generated by the [[Area Manager|Area Manager]] or [[General Manager|General Manager]] of the hotel. Each shift has up to 6 possible clock-ins: Clock-in, Lunch Out, Lunch In, Break Out, Break In, and Clock-out. The system automatically determines which type of clock-in corresponds based on the order of clock-ins already recorded for the shift. To clock in, the collaborator must have an active [[Timesheet]], which requires being enrolled in the [[Schedule]] with an active assignment (RR-C-03). When closing the shift (Clock-out), the system calculates gross hours, applies the lunch deduction according to the rules, and updates the Timesheet with net hours.

**Acceptance criteria:**
The collaborator must have an active Timesheet (RR-C-03); without an active Timesheet, the system blocks and shows: *"You have no active shift. Contact your supervisor"*. The QR must be valid and not expired; an invalid or expired QR blocks the action. The system identifies the type of clock-in based on the order of previous records for the shift; the order cannot be skipped. Duplicate clock-ins of the same type in the same shift are not allowed. If offline storage is active (RNF-C-02), the record is queued and synced when the connection is restored. Lunch deduction: lunch < 30 min → 30 min deducted (mandatory minimum); lunch ≥ 30 min → actual time deducted; no Lunch punch → auto-deduction of 30 min when closing the shift. Net hours = Gross hours − Lunch deduction − actual Breaks.

**Flow:**
`App → Attendance Section → Scan QR` → MANUAL → `Point camera at hotel QR` → AUTOMATIC → If QR valid and Timesheet active → `Identifies type of clock-in by order` → `Records clock-in with time and date` → `Shows confirmation: "Clock-in recorded at HH:MM" (or the corresponding clock-in type)` → `Shift Timesheet updated` / If Clock-out (end of shift) → `Calculates gross hours (Clock-out − Clock-in)` → `Applies lunch deduction` → `Calculates net hours` → `Confirms: "Clock-out recorded. Today's net hours: X.X hrs"` / If no active Timesheet → `Blocks · Shows: "You have no active shift. Contact your supervisor"` / If QR invalid → `Blocks · Shows: "Invalid QR code. Request an updated QR from your supervisor"` / If offline → `Queues record locally · Shows: "No connection. Your clock-in will be recorded when you recover signal"`
