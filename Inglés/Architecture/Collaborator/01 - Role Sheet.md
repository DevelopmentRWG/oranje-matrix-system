# ROLE SHEET — COLLABORATOR

---

| **Supervises**     | —                                                                           |

---

## Description

Central entity of the Oranje system. A person recruited, assigned, and operationally managed at hotels. They are the **passive subject** of the system: their lifecycle is controlled by other roles (Recruiter, Inspector, Area Manager). Their only autonomous state-change action is activating voluntary availability (Yellow).

---

## Objective in the System

Complete their app sign-up, register daily attendance via QR, report voluntary availability when applicable, report workplace accidents, and consult their own operational data (schedule, timesheet, pay, profile).

---

## Main Actions

- Complete sign-up data — Phase 2 (RF-C-01)
- Complete emergency data — Phase 3 (RF-C-02)
- Scan QR and clock in (6 punch types) (RF-C-03)
- Activate voluntary availability — Yellow (RF-C-04)
- Report workplace accident from the app (RF-C-05)
- View My Schedule — read-only (RF-C-06)
- View My Timesheet — read-only (RF-C-07)
- View My weekly Pay — read-only (RF-C-08)
- View notifications (RF-C-09)
- View My profile and status light status (RF-C-10)

---

## Key Permissions

View (own data only) · Create (sign-up, punches, accident report, activate Yellow) · Limited edit (own profile and emergency data)

---

## Access Level

🟢 Low (own data only)

---

## Device

Mobile + Web (responsive) — QR clock-in is exclusive to mobile; all other features available on both channels

---

## Usage Frequency

High — daily
