## Responsibilities

### What the Collaborator DOES

- **Complete sign-up (Phase 2):** enters position, English level, experience level, transportation type, and hiring modality (required fields); SSN, ITIN, and the SSN/ITIN document are optional. If neither SSN nor ITIN is provided, the system automatically activates a 16% retention on their pay (refundable) — see [[Deductions]].
- **Complete emergency data (Phase 3):** enters emergency contact (name, phone, relationship), blood type, and allergies/medical conditions.
- **Clock in via QR:** scans the QR generated by the Area Manager or General Manager and registers each of the 6 punches of the workday (Clock-in, Lunch Out, Lunch In, Break Out, Break In, Clock-out).
- **Activate Yellow:** activates voluntary availability from the app when in a rest period and wishes to be considered for temporary assignment. This is their only autonomous state-change action.
- **Report accident:** creates the initial workplace accident card from the app when they are the first to detect or experience the accident (Scenario A).
- **View My Schedule:** views their assigned shifts for the current and next week (read-only).
- **View My Timesheet:** views their gross hours, lunch deduction, net hours, and compliance status (read-only).
- **View My Pay:** views the **history of their already-released payments** (week, hotel, hours, amount paid, date); the amount of the current/upcoming payment **is not visible** (RR-C-05) (read-only).
- **View notifications:** receives system alerts about status changes, assignments, sign-up validations, and accident report updates.
- **View My Profile:** views their full profile, current status light state, and registered data.

### What the Collaborator does NOT do

- **Does NOT accept or reject assignments** — assignment is executed by the Recruiter or Group Leader.
- **Does NOT request reassignment** — if in Pink, they can activate Yellow (if applicable), but cannot directly request reassignment.
- **Does NOT authorize any action** from another role or from the system.
- **Does NOT assign anyone or manage other collaborators.**
- **Does NOT edit the Schedule** — it is read-only for them.
- **Does NOT correct punches** — punch correction is exclusive to the Area Manager.
- **Does NOT see other collaborators' data** — their visibility is restricted to their own records (RR-C-01).
- **Does NOT access management modules** (requisitions, pool, general accounting, QA).
- **Cannot clock in via web** — QR clock-in (RF-C-03) requires the phone camera and is exclusive to mobile (RR-C-04).

## Interactions with Other Roles

See source: [[Collaborator#Roles that interact with the Collaborator]]

| Role | How they interact with the Collaborator |
|---|---|
| [[Collaborator\|Recruiter]] | Recruits them, captures Phase 1, validates Phase 2/3, assigns them to a hotel; manages their status in the Pool |
| Recruitment Manager | Oversees Blacklist cases; does not intervene in the Collaborator's daily operations |
| Area Manager | Generates the access QR, manages their Schedule, corrects punches, can put them in Pink or Red |
| Supervisor | Can report accident Scenario B, can put them in Pink or Red |
| General Manager | Generates QR, can put them in Pink or Red; global hotel visibility |
| [[Inspector]] | Verifies arrival on Day 1, delivers uniform on Day 3+, investigates Red cases, closes accident cards |
