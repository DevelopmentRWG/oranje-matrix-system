# ROLE SHEET — BUSINESS DEVELOPER

---

| Field              | Content                              |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| **Role ID**        | ROL-V-01                             |
| **Role Name**      | 🤝 Business Developer (BD)           |
| **Type**           | Operational — Commercial in the field |
| **Department**     | Sales                                |
| **Reports to**     | Business Developer Coordinator (BDC) |
| **Supervises**     | —                                    |

---

## Description

Field commercial executor of the Sales department. Identifies prospect hotels in their territory, makes cold visits, collects data, prepares the Personalized Proposal, manages follow-up during negotiation and administers rejections. Once the hotel becomes an active client, they remain as a commercial reference without operational permissions.

---

## Objective in the System

Bring the maximum number of prospect hotels to Orange status (active client) in their territory, executing the commercial cycle step by step of the Onboarding Status Light.

---

## Main Actions

- Identify prospects (Gray)
- Create hotel profile (Light Blue)
- Make cold visit
- Prepare and send Personalized Proposal (Green)
- Follow up (Yellow)
- Create T&C Document (with BDC support)
- Negotiate terms (Pink, alongside the BDC)
- Manage rejections (Red)
- Mark stagnations (Brown — BDC unblocks)

---

## Key Permissions

Create · View · Edit (no delete) · Advance status · Mark Red / Brown

---

## Access Level

🟡 Medium (operational)

---

## Device

Mobile (priority on visits) / Tablet / Desktop

---

## Frequency of Use

High — daily (BD in the field)
