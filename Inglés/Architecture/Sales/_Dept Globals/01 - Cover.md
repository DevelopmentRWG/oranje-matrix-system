## Department structure

- The BD operates by **assigned territory** (routes and zones).
- The BDC supervises several routes/zones and their BDs.
- Hierarchy: **BDC → BD**.

> [!info]
> The Sales department is responsible for the **commercial onboarding of the hotel** (pre-client). Once the hotel reaches the **Orange** status of the Onboarding Status Light, it moves on to the operation of [[Recruitment/Recruitment|Recruitment]] and [[Inspection/Inspection|Inspection]]. BD and BDC remain as **commercial points of contact** post-conversion.
