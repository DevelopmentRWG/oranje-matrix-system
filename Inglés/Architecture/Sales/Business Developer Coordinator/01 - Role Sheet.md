# ROLE SHEET — BUSINESS DEVELOPER COORDINATOR

---

| **Role ID**        | ROL-V-02                                   |
| **Role Name**      | 🧑‍💼 Business Developer Coordinator (BDC) |
| **Type**           | Supervisor — Commercial                    |
| **Department**     | Sales                                      |
| **Reports to**     | Management                                 |
| **Supervises**     | Business Developers (BDs) of its territory |

---

## Description

Supervisor role of the Sales department. Supervises the entire panorama of the routes and zones assigned to it, follows up on the BD, **validates the terms and conditions** before closing the Contract and **approves the conversion** of the prospect into an active client. It is the **final yes** of the negotiation. Manages special cases (Brown stagnation, paused Black clients).

---

## Objective in the System

Ensure that each conversion meets the commercial criteria, validate the T&C, supervise the team's performance and keep the Pipeline flow healthy through the unblocking of special cases.

---

## Main Actions

- Validate T&C Document (final yes)
- Create Hotel User
- Approve conversion to client
- Negotiate terms (Pink, alongside the BD)
- Create T&C Document (support to the BD)
- Unblock stagnation (Brown)
- Manage paused client (Black) and reactivate
- Supervise BDs and generate executive reports
