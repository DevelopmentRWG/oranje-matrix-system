# General Manager

Role of highest authority within the hotel. Supervises the [[Hotel/Area Manager|Area Managers]] of the hotel's operational areas. The General Manager **always exists** in both hierarchies (see [[Hotel/Hotel|Hotel]]).

> [!note] Simple hierarchy
> In hotels with a simple hierarchy, the General Manager also operates as [[Hotel/Area Manager|Area Manager]] (same person, two roles). In an extended hierarchy, they are different people.

## Responsibilities

### Requisitions

- Creates staffing [[Requisition|requisitions]].
- **Approves** requisitions (**Authorized** status).
- **Rejects** requisitions, returning them to the creator with observations (**In progress** status).

### Management of assigned staff

- Generates the **QR** code so that collaborators can clock in on the [[Timesheet]].
- Sends collaborators to rest (**Pink — Stand-by** status in the [[Collaborator Status Light|Collaborator Status Light]]).
- Reports collaborators (**Red — Reported** status in the [[Collaborator Status Light|Collaborator Status Light]]).
- Reports [[Core/Modules/Workplace Accident/Workplace Accident|workplace accidents]] detected on the property.

### General supervision

- Global visibility of the [[Core/Modules/Schedule|Schedule]] and [[Timesheet]] of all [[Hotel Departments|departments]].
- Main point of contact with Oranje at the executive level.
