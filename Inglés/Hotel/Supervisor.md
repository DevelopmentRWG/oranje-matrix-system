# Supervisor

Operational role of the [[Hotel/Hotel|Hotel]] module, subordinate to the [[Hotel/Area Manager|Area Manager]]. Responsible for creating the staffing [[Requisition|requisitions]] that the hotel needs to cover.

> [!note] Extended hierarchy
> In hotels with an extended hierarchy, this role corresponds to the Supervisor of the [[Hotel Departments|department]], subordinate to the [[Hotel/Area Manager|Area Manager]] of its department. The platform responsibilities are the same.

## Responsibilities

### Requisitions

- Creates the [[Requisition|Requisition]] from the app, specifying:
  - Required [[Posiciones|positions]].
  - Number of people per position.
  - Start date.
  - Schedule.
  - [[Employment Types|Hiring modality]].
  - [[English Levels|English level]] preference.
  - Additional notes.
- Sends the requisition to the [[Hotel/Area Manager|Area Manager]] for approval.

> [!note] About authorization
> The Supervisor **cannot authorize** a requisition. Every requisition must be reviewed and approved by the [[Hotel/Area Manager|Area Manager]] or the [[Hotel/General Manager|General Manager]] before reaching the [[Recruitment/Recruitment|Recruitment]] team.

### Management of assigned staff

- Sends collaborators to rest (**Pink — Stand-by** status in the [[Collaborator Status Light|Collaborator Status Light]]).
- Reports collaborators (**Red — Reported** status in the [[Collaborator Status Light|Collaborator Status Light]]).
- Reports [[Core/Modules/Workplace Accident/Workplace Accident|workplace accidents]] detected on the property (see [[Core/Modules/Workplace Accident/Workplace Accident Flow|Workplace Accident Flow]]).
- Physically goes to the scene of the incident and captures the on-site information on the accident card: exact location, circumstances, witnesses and immediate care provided.
