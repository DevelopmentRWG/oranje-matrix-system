# QA Manager

Role responsible for the quality strategy and supervision of the operators team within the [[QA/QA|QA]] department.

## Responsibilities

- **Defines the metrics and KPIs** that each [[QA Operator|QA Operator]] must monitor per department. See [[QA/Metrics and KPIs by Department|Metrics and KPIs by Department]].
- **Supervises the 5 QA operators**, one for each department: Inspection, Hotel, Collaborator, Sales and Recruitment.
- **Consolidates the observations** from all operators into a global view of operational quality.
- **Presents quality reports** to management with findings, trends and areas for improvement.
- **Updates the [[Quality Indicator|Quality Indicator]]** of each department based on the operators' reports.
- **Escalates critical problems** when a department remains in the Red state of the [[Quality Indicator|Quality Indicator]] without improvement.
