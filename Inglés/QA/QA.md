# QA

Department in charge of supervising the operational quality of all Oranje departments. Each department has an assigned [[QA Operator|QA Operator]] that monitors performance metrics and issues observations for continuous improvement.

> [!info]
> QA does not execute the operation of any department. Its function is to **observe, measure and provide feedback** so that each area keeps its quality within the defined standards.
