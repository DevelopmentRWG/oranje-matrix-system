# Timesheet

Record of time worked by a collaborator at a hotel. It is created from the [[Core/Modules/Schedule|Schedule]] and is fed with the collaborator's clock-ins during the week.

## Origin

- Each collaborator enrolled in the hotel's [[Core/Modules/Schedule|Schedule]] has their own timesheet.
- The schedule is what opens the door to the creation of the timesheet.

## Clock-in

The collaborator clocks in via **QR** generated by the [[Hotel/Area Manager|Area Manager]]. Clock-ins are recorded in entry/exit pairs for each period:

- **Clock In**
- **Lunch**
- **Clock Out**

This counts the collaborator's worked time.

## Use by the hotel

In another part of the panel, the hotel can see how much it will have to pay at the end of the week for all the worked hours recorded in the timesheets.

## Lunch Deduction

The system applies a lunch deduction to the computation of the collaborator's hours on each shift. After 6 continuous hours of work, the collaborator must take their lunch.

### Deduction rule

| Scenario                | Deduction applied        |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Lunch < 30 min           | 30 min (minimum)          |
| Lunch ≥ 30 min           | Actual time taken         |
| No Lunch clock-in        | 30 min (auto-deduction)  |

> [!info] The rule applies to **all** collaborators without exception. See [[Business Rules#Deducción de Lunch|Business Rules#Lunch Deduction]] for the formal definition.

### Payable hours calculation

- **Gross hours** = Clock Out − Clock In
- **Net hours** = Gross hours − Lunch Deduction
- The lunch deduction applies on every shift without exception

> [!note] The current mechanism records a single **Lunch** clock-in. To calculate the actual duration of the lunch, the system needs to capture the start and end of the period (two clock-ins or automatic logic). Pending consideration for development.

### Extended Lunch Indicator

The system automatically flags collaborators whose lunch exceeds 30 minutes.

- **Who sees it:** [[Inspector]], [[Inspection/Coordinator|Coordinator]], [[Recruitment Manager|Recruitment Manager]]
- **Who does NOT see it:** [[Hotel/Area Manager|Area Manager]], [[Hotel/Supervisor|Supervisor]]
- **What it shows:** collaborator, hotel, date, actual lunch time
- **When it activates:** when the lunch time records more than 30 minutes

> [!important] This indicator is an internal Oranje supervision tool. The hotel does not have visibility into the individual lunch times of the collaborators.
