# Contract

Formal agreement between Oranje and the hotel that results from the negotiation closing in [[Onboarding Status Light|Onboarding Status Pink]]. It formalizes the conditions agreed in the [[Terms and Conditions Document|Terms and Conditions Document]] and defines the operational parameters of the relationship.

## Contents

- **Pay rate** — pay agreed for the collaborator. It is applied per hotel: if the collaborator works at multiple hotels, each one has its own pay rate according to its contract.
- **Bill rate** — charge agreed with the hotel.
- **Overtime** — agreed overtime policy.
- **Holidays** — agreed handling and surcharges.
- **Week start and end** — the hotel's weekly configuration for the [[Schedule]].
- **Validity** — contract validity period.
- **Renewal** — renewal conditions.

## Lifecycle

- **Formalized in:** [[Onboarding Status Light|Onboarding Status Pink]] after validation by the [[Business Developer Coordinator]].
- **Prior input:** [[Terms and Conditions Document|Terms and Conditions Document]].
