# Blacklist

List of banned collaborators who cannot be recruited again.

## What it is

Record of people who have been blocked due to:

- Absences.
- Serious offenses committed at the hotel.

## Use in Recruitment

For [[Recruiter|recruiters]], knowing who is on the blacklist serves to:

- **Avoid re-recruiting** someone banned when they apply.
- **Keep a record** of blocked collaborators.

## How someone enters the blacklist

A collaborator transitions to the **Black** state in the [[Collaborator Status Light|Collaborator Status Light]] due to:

- **Serious offense (manual)** → any role in the Recruitment department ([[Recruiter|Recruiter]], [[Recruitment/Recruiters Group Leader|Group Leader]] or [[Recruitment Manager|Recruitment Manager]]) can add them with mandatory **reason and evidence**; an auditable record is kept.
- **3 absences** → automatic blacklist by the system.
- **Dispute resolved in favor of the hotel** → after a hotel report (Red state) and investigation by the [[Inspector]].

## Investigation process

When a collaborator enters the Red state (hotel report), the [[Inspector]] of the zone investigates the dispute.

> [!info] The Inspector has their own authority to decide the outcome. They do not require escalation to the Recruitment Manager to issue the resolution.

- **Resolution in favor of the hotel:** the collaborator transitions to **Black** ([[Core/Modules/Blacklist|Blacklist]]).
- **Resolution in favor of the collaborator:** the collaborator returns to **Strong Green** (available).

## Permanence of the Black state

> [!warning] Black is PERMANENT. There is no rehabilitation process nor any appeal instance.

The collaborator's record is kept in the system under the following conditions:

- The collaborator is **not deleted** from the system.
- They **do not appear in active recruitment searches**.
- The **history is kept** intact for internal reference.
