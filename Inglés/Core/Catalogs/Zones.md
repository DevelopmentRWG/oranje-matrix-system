# Zones

Geographic division of the state into operational areas. Each zone is the basis for assigning [[Inspector|inspectors]] to hotels.

## List

- Center
- South
- East
- West
- Northwest
- Southeast

> [!note]
> List based on the cardinal points and their derivatives. It may include other cardinal combinations not explicitly listed here.

## Usage

- **Each hotel belongs to a zone.**
- **Each zone is assigned an [[Inspector]].**
- Because of this relationship, the inspector of the hotel's zone is responsible for following up on any dispute that occurs at that hotel — which is why the Inspector appears in the header of the [[Requisition|Requisition]].
