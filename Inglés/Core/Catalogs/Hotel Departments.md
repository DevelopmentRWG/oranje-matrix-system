# Hotel Departments

Catalog of the hotel's internal departments for which Oranje recruits collaborators.

## List

- **Housekeeping** — Cleaning and maintenance of rooms and public areas.
- **Food** — Preparation and service of food and beverages.
- **Maintenance** — Repairs, installations and upkeep of the property.
- **Front Desk** — Reception, guest service and lobby operations.
