# English Levels

English proficiency levels used in Oranje. They apply both to the **preference** the hotel indicates in the [[Requisition|Requisition]] and to the level declared by the collaborator at sign-up.

## Levels

- Basic
- Intermediate
- Advanced
- Conversational
