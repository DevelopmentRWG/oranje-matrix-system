# Timesheet

Record of hours worked by an associate at a hotel. It is created from the [[Core/Módulos/Schedule|Schedule]] and populated with the associate's punch records throughout the week.

## Origin

- Each associate enrolled in the hotel's [[Core/Módulos/Schedule|Schedule]] has their own timesheet.
- The schedule is what enables timesheet creation.
- If the [[Core/Módulos/Schedule|Schedule]] changes after the timesheet was created, the timesheet is automatically updated to reflect the changes.
- Only associates with an active assignment (fixed or temporary) appear in the [[Core/Módulos/Schedule|Schedule]]; without a Schedule no Timesheet is generated, and without a Timesheet punching is not possible.

## Work Day

- The daily shift is **8 hours**
- The work week is 7 days: **5 working + 2 rest**
- Gross weekly total: **40 hours** (8 hrs x 5 days)
- Net payable weekly total: **37.5 hours** (40 hrs - 30 min lunch x 5 shifts)

## Punching

The associate punches via **QR** generated by the [[Hotel/Manager de Área|Area Manager]]. Punches are recorded in entry/exit pairs for each period:

- **Clock In** — shift start
- **Lunch Out** — leaves for lunch
- **Lunch In** — returns from lunch
- **Break Out** — leaves for break
- **Break In** — returns from break
- **Clock Out** — shift end

This tracks the associate's worked time.

## Hotel-Side View

In another section of the panel, the hotel can see how much they will need to pay at the end of the week for all worked hours recorded in the timesheets.

## Weekly Summary

- At the end of the week, the associate's Timesheets across all hotels are aggregated in the [[Contabilidad/Consolidado Semanal del Colaborador|Weekly Associate Summary]]
- If the associate worked at multiple hotels (via temporary assignment), each Timesheet contributes its net hours at the corresponding contract's pay rate

## Lunch Deduction

The system applies a lunch deduction to the associate's hour computation for each shift.

### Deduction Rule

| Scenario               | Deduction Applied        |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Lunch < 30 min          | 30 min (minimum)        |
| Lunch >= 30 min         | Actual time taken       |
| No Lunch punch          | 30 min (auto-deduction) |
| Break recorded          | Actual break time       |

> [!info] This rule applies to **all** associates without exception. See [[Reglas de Negocio#Lunch Deduction]] for the formal definition.

### Payable Hours Calculation

- **Gross hours** = Clock Out - Clock In
- **Net hours** = Gross hours - Actual lunch - Actual breaks
- The lunch deduction applies every shift without exception

### Break Deduction

The system deducts break time from the associate's hour computation for each shift.

- Breaks are recorded with the **Break Out / Break In** pair
- The number and duration of breaks is defined by each hotel
- The deducted time is the actual time recorded between each pair of break punches

### Extended Lunch Indicator

The system automatically flags associates whose lunch exceeds 30 minutes.

- **Who sees it:** [[Inspector]], [[Inspección/Coordinador|Coordinator]], [[Manager de Reclutamiento]]
- **Who does NOT see it:** [[Hotel/Manager de Área|Area Manager]], [[Hotel/Supervisor|Supervisor]]
- **What it shows:** associate, hotel, date, actual lunch time
- **When it triggers:** when lunch time exceeds 30 minutes

> [!important] This indicator is an internal Oranje supervision tool. The hotel has no visibility into individual associate lunch times.
